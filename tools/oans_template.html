Map settings

With automatic detail on, stand labels show at or inside the threshold; buildings show outside it when enabled. Turn automatic detail off to control both layers manually.

Taxi route & runways

Choose a runway, select Draw on map, then click taxiways in order. Their names are added automatically and the route updates after each click. Drag the map to pan; a drag will not add a taxiway. You can edit the clearance directly. The aircraft's live position starts the route.
Runways shown are for the airport currently loaded on the map.
